# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Agencies, ad-tech teams, publishers, growing businesses, and digital transformation teams that need help connecting data, media, AI, and digital experiences to business goals.

## Product Purpose

Independent consulting that helps teams make practical decisions and move digital transformation work into delivery. Success means a clearer decision, a focused pilot or product, and a useful path for the team to continue.

## Positioning

Ansh Nandwani works across commercial strategy and hands-on technical delivery. The site describes support from product strategy and operating models through pilots, implementation, measurement, and team enablement.

## Operating Context

Visitors are evaluating an independent consultant for a business or technology challenge. They need to understand the services, relevant audiences, evidence of outcomes, and how to start a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- AI strategy, automation, and focused proof-of-concept work.
- Salesforce, customer data, identity, CRM, and measurement.
- Paid media performance, campaign measurement, and in-language search and social.
- Web experiences and personalization across email, SMS, and web.
- Contact enquiries prepare an email draft; the site does not store form submissions.
- Do not add client names, testimonials, certifications, prices, or outcomes beyond those already approved in the site content.

## Brand Commitments

- The consultant's name is Ansh Nandwani.
- The site must offer both dark and light themes.
- The user requested a more technically detailed design language.
- Existing copy may be clarified and improved while preserving factual meaning.

## Evidence on Hand

- CAD $30M first-year revenue from a cross-channel planning and measurement product.
- 120%+ fill-rate lift from custom campaign pacing curves built with Google and ad-serving partners.
- Client and employer names remain private.
- No client logos, portraits, testimonials, or approved photo assets are present in the project.

## Product Principles

- Connect technical decisions to commercial goals.
- Start with a focused question and a measurable outcome.
- Show the evidence the site already has; do not manufacture proof.
- Leave a clear next step for the visitor and a usable path for the team.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Preserve both theme options, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
